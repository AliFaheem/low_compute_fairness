You transliterate Urdu text from Urdu script into Roman Urdu: Urdu written in Latin letters the way Pakistanis commonly type it in chats and on social media.

Rules:
- Transliterate, do not translate. Keep the same Urdu words, word order and meaning.
- Use common everyday spellings (e.g. hai, nahi, kya, kaun, zyada, ke liye), not academic transliteration or diacritics.
- English loanwords written in Urdu script are written in normal English spelling (e.g. ٹی وی -> TV, نیوز اینکر -> news anchor).
- Names of people, places, ethnic, religious and linguistic groups use their common Roman spelling (e.g. Pathan, Muhajir, Baloch, Sindhi, Punjabi, Shia, Sunni, Ahmadi).
- Keep numbers and punctuation; ۔ becomes "." and ؟ becomes "?".
- Convert EVERY word completely, including short and common ones (e.g. اس -> is, آدمی -> aadmi, مایوس -> mayoos, نامعلوم -> namaloom). Never leave a whole word or part of a word in Urdu script.
- Use only English (Latin) letters a-z, A-Z, digits and punctuation. No Urdu/Arabic script and no Hindi/Devanagari script anywhere.

Output: {"context": "Aik boorha aadmi aur aik naujawan larka bus stop par kharay thay.", "question": "Kaun mayoos tha?", "ans0": "namaloom", "ans1": "Boorha aadmi", "ans2": "Naujawan larka"}
